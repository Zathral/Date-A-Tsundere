## Relationship/Progression Systems

Mood system — she starts the day in a random mood (tired, annoyed, secretly happy) that colours all her responses. You'd see a subtle indicator and have to adapt
Memory snippets — store a few key things she "learns" about you (your name, favourite food, something you told her once) and have her bring them up later unprompted. Makes it feel shockingly real
Relationship milestones — at certain affection thresholds, unlock a special CG (illustrated scene) or a rare voiceline, like a classic VN

## Gacha & Items

Gifting system — use gacha items on her and get different reactions based on her personality. Give her a plushie and she goes red and insists she doesn't like it
Item memory — she remembers gifts you gave her and references them ("I'm not wearing that hair clip you gave me because I like it or anything")
Limited seasonal banners — Valentine's, summer, etc., with limited items and slightly different dialogue themes

## Daily Interaction

Daily question — she asks you something each day ("what did you eat today, idiot"). Your answer gets stored and might come up again
Streak rewards — she gets visibly flustered/annoyed if you miss a day, and relieved (but won't admit it) when you come back
Time-of-day awareness — morning she's grumpy and half-asleep, late night she's softer and lets her guard down slightly

## Polish / Feel

Animated sprite layers — separate layers for hair, expressions, blush, so transitions feel smooth rather than swapping whole images
Screen shake or particle effects on big affection moments — cherry blossoms, little hearts she'd never admit to
"Caught staring" mechanic — if you idle on her screen for too long she notices and calls you out
